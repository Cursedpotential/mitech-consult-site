TraceIQ grew from a need to make a large, complicated body of information understandable. The engineering problem involves ingestion, normalization, search, timelines, relationships, provenance, and review—not simply generating answers.

The architecture has changed repeatedly as the data model and operating constraints became clearer. That rebuilding is part of the project: each version has made the boundaries between source material, derived analysis, and human judgment more explicit.

Private source material and the personal circumstances behind the project are intentionally outside this archive.
