Electrical work brought the physical layer back into focus: how power is distributed, how installations are laid out, and how traditional electrical practices meet increasingly networked building systems.

The work included residential and light-commercial installations, power distribution, service work, wiring, conduit, and coordination with low-voltage systems.

## What I took from it

Software and controls can make a system look intelligent, but every one of those systems still depends on sound physical work. Learning from experienced tradespeople reinforced the value of fundamentals, sequencing, and building something that can be safely maintained.
